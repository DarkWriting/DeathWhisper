# Horror atmosphere and world content (proposals)

These are **ideas only**, not yet implemented. They are deliberately unrelated to active powers and avoid destructive world changes. All should be gated by gamerules and bounded server-side.

1. Door knocks and window scratches that stop the moment a player looks outside
2. Old tape recorders with original recordings that tell fragments of a mystery
3. Haunted portraits whose faces subtly change between visits
4. Silhouettes seen through windows only during storms
5. Abandoned mine radios with strange transmissions and static
6. Footprints outside bases leading to nowhere
7. Ambient wildlife going silent just before a rare event
8. Mist patches and unnatural fog in forests without altering world blocks
9. Fictional missing-person posters and lost-item notices in villages
10. A scattered investigation journal connecting clues across dimensions
11. Run-down research facilities with environmental storytelling
12. Rare wandering strangers who vanish behind corners
13. Mirrors with delayed or incorrect reflections
14. Unmarked graves that reveal stories, not just treasure
15. Quiet haunted houses with creaking floorboards and distant footsteps
16. Anomaly hunts where players must spot the one altered detail in a room
17. Abandoned phone booths that ring with short cryptic messages
18. Cooperative ghost investigations using recording and detection equipment
19. Personalized but non-invasive hauntings that reference explored places
20. A pacing director to prevent repeated or predictable jumpscares

Recommended first batch: recorder mysteries, environmental silence, village rumours/posters, loaded-chunk atmospheric phenomena and cooperative anomaly hunts.
